Sound effects in this folder come from Kenney's "Interface Sounds" pack (v1.0).

    Source:  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
    Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
             http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 places the work in the public domain: free to use in personal, educational
and commercial projects, with no attribution required. Kenney asks only that
you credit him if you feel like it — hence this file.

The originals are Ogg Vorbis, converted here to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 (96 kbps) so
Web Audio's decodeAudioData handles them everywhere, iOS included. All five
together cost about 28 KB.

These were picked by measurement rather than by ear. The number below each is
the spectral centroid — the centre of mass of the sound's energy, and a decent
proxy for how harsh it is. The pack ranges from 406 Hz (a soft thud) up to
12 kHz (a piercing tick), and anything that fires on every single answer has to
sit at the bottom of that range.

    correct.mp3  <- bong_001.ogg          0.12s    507 Hz   every correct answer
    wrong.mp3    <- minimize_008.ogg      0.23s    482 Hz   its falling counterpart
    streak.mp3   <- maximize_008.ogg      0.23s    406 Hz   warmest in the pack
    level.mp3    <- confirmation_001.ogg  0.29s    823 Hz   level up
    win.mp3      <- confirmation_004.ogg  0.49s   1587 Hz   clean round / best

The first set was chosen by filename alone and was wrong: level.mp3 was
confirmation_002 at 3017 Hz, and everything else was a WebAudio oscillator —
a bare triangle beep on every correct answer. There is no synthesis left.

To swap any of them, drop a replacement in with the same name, or point
SAMPLES in lib/juice.js at a different file.
